# Welcome to Nooremberg

> *"Press start to begin your journey."*

This site has been around for a while. The pixel-art bones haven't changed, but the inside has been rebuilt. Here's a quick tour of what's new.

## What's new

- **Modern stack, no build step.** Plain HTML + ES modules + modern CSS. Ships straight to GitHub Pages, no Webpack stress.
- **Skill cards.** Each technology is a flippable trading card with rarity, XP, mastery, and mana stats. Click one — there's lore on the back.
- **Quest log.** Work history reframed as quests with status, rewards, and earned XP.
- **Library (this blog).** Posts live as Markdown files in `/content/posts/` and are rendered client-side.
- **HUD.** A sticky top bar with HP, level, clock, and a day/night toggle.
- **Achievements.** Eight in total. Some are obvious. Most are not.

## Easter eggs (no spoilers, mostly)

There's a Konami code. There's a console you can open with `/`. There's a chest somewhere. There may be more.

## Why pixel art

Because it's honest. Every pixel is intentional. Constraints are a feature, not a bug. Same with the rest of life, probably.
