How to play

  • Use arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen controls to move.
  • Collect blooms to fill the level goal and increase your score.
  • Shades move after you. If one lands on you, the run ends.
  • Dark wells block movement for both you and the shades.
  • Use Bloom Burst once per level to grow extra blooms and buy time.
