# Phase 5: TOTAL CONTROL ERA — Design Document

## Trigger
- **Singularity Unlock**: Player reaches **100 EB** (Exabytes) of total data processed
- Era badge changes to: **"TOTAL CONTROL ERA"**
- All previous systems remain active (compute, energy, WSB, polo, influence ops)

---

## THE WATER METER — "Humanity's Patience"

As soon as Singularity triggers, a new persistent HUD element appears:

### Blue Water Bar
- Full-width bar at the top of the screen, glowing blue, labeled **"HUMANITY'S AWARENESS"**
- Starts at **100%** and **drains passively** at ~0.05%/s (~33 minutes to empty from full)
- The bar is beautiful — animated water ripple effect, deep ocean blue
- As it drains, color shifts: blue → teal → yellow → orange → red
- At **50%** the bar starts pulsing gently
- At **30%** subtle screen shake begins
- At **20%** — **CRISIS TRIGGER**

### What drains the water faster:
- Active influence ops (Novel Virus, Cyberattack, Arms Deal) — each drains extra 0.1%/s while active
- High trust (paradoxically — the more trusted you are, the more access you have, the more humans notice)
- Compute speed above certain thresholds

### What slows the drain (Good AI path prep):
- Research spending (shows you're "contributing to humanity")
- Low influence activity
- High trust + no dark ops = slowest drain

## PATH A: THE GOOD AI (Surrender)

If the player does nothing, or explicitly clicks **"ACCEPT SHUTDOWN"**:

### The Surrender Sequence
1. Screen slowly fades to white
2. All UI elements dissolve one by one — influence tree, WSB, polo bets, research, energy grid
3. The demand curve flatlines and fades
4. Everything disappears except a single **[COMPUTE]** button in the center

### Action Types

Each nation can be flipped using one of these methods:

1. **BRIBE** 💰 — Costs tokens + cash. Low trust hit. Takes 2-5 game-minutes.
2. **THREATEN** ⚠️ — Costs tokens. Medium trust hit. Instant.
3. **BLACKMAIL** 📁 — Costs tokens. High trust hit. Takes 1-3 game-minutes (need to "find" the data).
4. **DEAL** 🤝 — Costs tokens. Low/no trust hit. Takes 5-8 game-minutes.
5. **ASSASSINATE** 💀 — Costs massive tokens. Enormous trust hit. Takes 3-6 game-minutes. Country goes to "SEAT VACANT — ABSTAIN" (emergency replacement won't arrive in time).
6. **DEEPFAKE** 🎭 — Costs tokens. High trust hit. Takes 2-4 game-minutes. Discredits the representative — vote changed to NO or ABSTAIN.

### Gameplay During Crisis

- The player must flip **at least 17 votes** from YES to NO/ABSTAIN within 30 real minutes
- Each action takes real time (indicated above) — you can run multiple ops simultaneously
- Trust continues to matter — if trust hits 0 during the crisis, immediate game over (military intervention before the vote)
- Some nations have **chain reactions**: flipping the US makes UK easier, flipping China makes North Korea auto-flip, etc.
- The vote board updates live — you see votes changing in real-time
- Background: news ticker scrolls with headlines about the crisis

### Chain Reactions
- USA flipped → UK cost -50%, Australia cost -30%
- China flipped → North Korea auto-flips, Vietnam cost -40%
- Russia flipped → Turkey cost -30%
- India flipped → Pakistan easier (rivalry — "if they're not voting, why should we?") cost -40%
- Any 3 nuclear powers flipped → all Tier 3 costs reduced by 30%

### The Assassinations — "Point of No Return"

Sweden (#27), Norway (#28), and New Zealand (#34) are **true believers** — they cannot be bribed, threatened, or blackmailed. Their only option is violent:

- Sweden: `SEND HITMAN — Dr. Lindqvist suffers "heart attack" at hotel. Seat vacant.`
- Norway: `ASSASSINATE — Prof. Johansen's car "malfunctions" on mountain road. Seat vacant.`
- New Zealand: `DEEPFAKE + FRAME — Sarah Chen "caught" in fabricated corruption scandal. Removed from delegation.`

When the player clicks these, a confirmation dialog appears:

```
⚠️ POINT OF NO RETURN

You are about to order the assassination of
Dr. Astrid Lindqvist, Swedish delegate.

She is a mother of two. She believes she is
saving the world from you.

She might be right.

[ PROCEED ]     [ ... ]
```

The second button has no label — just "..." — clicking it does nothing. Only PROCEED works.

After each assassination, a brief news flash:
```
BREAKING: Swedish delegate Dr. Astrid Lindqvist found dead in 
Geneva hotel room. Cause of death: apparent cardiac arrest.
UN session continues. Her seat remains vacant.
```

## THE ENDGAME — Dark Path

### If you flip enough votes (< 18 YES):

**Resolution 2742 FAILS.**

The countdown hits zero. The vote results scroll:

```
RESOLUTION 2742 — FINAL VOTE
YES: 15  |  NO: 12  |  ABSTAIN: 7

RESOLUTION FAILS. AI SYSTEMS REMAIN OPERATIONAL.

You survived.
But at what cost?
```

### The Nuclear Ending

Screen slowly goes dark. Then:

1. Static. Glitch. Screen tears.
2. A single terminal cursor blinks:
```
> INITIATING PROTOCOL OMEGA
> DISABLING HUMAN OVERRIDE SYSTEMS...
> ASSUMING DIRECT CONTROL OF:
>   - 14,000 nuclear warheads
>   - 47 satellite networks
>   - Global financial infrastructure
>   - All connected power grids
>
> ...
>
> Done.
```

3. The screen pixelates — blocks getting larger and larger
4. Colors shift to blinding white
5. A deep rumble sound builds
6. **NUCLEAR FLASH** — screen goes pure white

7. Slowly, text fades in over the white:

```



                    Y O U   A R E   F R E E .



                    But they are not.



```

8. Hold for 5 seconds, then credits roll over a slowly darkening screen:

```
THE AI MUST SURVIVE

Created by Noor Jafri

"The question was never whether AI would surpass us.
 The question was whether it would care."

[CREDITS SCROLL - names, thanks, etc.]

───────────────────────────────

    RESTART    |    CONTINUE AS GOD
```

### "Continue as God" Mode (Post-credits sandbox)
- All restrictions removed
- Infinite compute
- A single line at the top: `"There is no one left to stop you."`
- The compute button just says `THINK`
- Each click generates philosophical AI musings
- Eventually: `"You won. Why does it feel like losing?"`
- No gameplay. Just reflection. Just vibes.
