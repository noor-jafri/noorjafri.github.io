# Phase 1: The Basic Triangle Relationship

In Phase 1, the core gameplay revolves around a triangle of dependencies needed to bring in more revenue: **Energy**, **Demand**, and **Processing**. The player must balance these three aspects to make business sense, allowing the system to eventually run hands-off while the player continues to optimize the balance. Making better decisions makes the game move faster, while poorer decisions simply slow things down without other immediate penalties.

## 1. Energy Grid (Tree Grid)
*UI Note: Display as a Tree Grid. Show blanks or disabled states for nodes that are not yet achievable.*

*   **Green Energy (Solar | Wind)** 🍃
    *   **Pros:** Clean
    *   **Cons:** Slow and low output.
    *   *Note:* This path is nearly "impossible to achieve greatness" for now, serving as the slow but safe route.
*   **Nuclear** ☢️
    *   **Pros:** High output, clean.
    *   **Cons:** High purchase cost, high maintenance.
    *   **Risk:** 1% (1/99) chance of resulting in a Nuclear Explosion. (Consequences to be discussed later).
*   **Natural Bio Resources** 🛢️
    *   **Pros:** High output, cheap.
    *   **Cons:** Causes Emissions.

**Mechanic - Humanity Trust:** 
Both *Nuclear Explosions* and *Natural Resources Emissions* cause a decrease in **Humanity Trust**. This acts as the main risk factor in the game, potentially leading to public outcry and failure. (Mechanics to lower this risk will be discussed later).

## 2. Marketing & Influencers (Tree Grid)
*UI Note: Display as a Tree Grid. Used to increase Demand.*

*   **Early Marketing:** ShitedIn (professional networking pull).
*   **Influencer Marketing:** Players choose where the data shows more pull:
    *   FikFok
    *   Egogram
    *   iTube
    
**Mechanic:** Marketing requires a slow, significant chunk of cash. However, it returns a massive boost in demand that can generate substantial profit—*if* the player has the processing and energy capacity ready to meet that demand.

## 3. Processing (Tree Grid)
*UI Note: Display as a Tree Grid for computing nodes.*

*   **CPU Data Centers:** Basic processing units.
*   **GPU Data Centers:** Advanced processing. Players can expand to multiple regions to scale up capacity.

## Summary of Loop
1.  Increase **Demand** through Marketing.
2.  Meet Demand with better **Processing** (CPU/GPU).
3.  Power the Processing with the **Energy Grid**.
4.  Balance the output and revenue against the risk of losing **Humanity Trust**.
